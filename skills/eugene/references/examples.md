# Worked examples of TASK mode

## Example 1: a GoHighLevel fix

**The user types:** "/eugene new leads from the website form are not getting the welcome text"

**EUGENE shows the brief:**
- MY TASK: make new website-form leads receive the welcome text.
- WHO USES IT: the client's new leads.
- HOW IT WORKS TODAY: the form saves the lead, but no text goes out.
- WHAT "DONE" LOOKS LIKE: every new lead gets the text within 1 minute.
- WHAT I HAVE: ? (asks for a screenshot of the workflow and its history)
- MY LIMITS: assumed: do not publish without your OK, test on your own contact.

**THINK FIRST (short):**
1. Likely causes: workflow in draft, wrong trigger form, the number has do-not-disturb, or the
   texting number is not registered (A2P).
2. Goal: 100% of new form leads texted within 1 minute.
3. How: read the workflow history for one lead, find the failed step, fix only that, test.
4. Proof: submit the form with your own contact and receive the text.
5. Risks: texting old leads again by mistake, texting people who replied STOP.

**Then:** plan, wait for yes, test on the user's contact, show proof, and close with:
"Next time you can say: 'New form leads get no welcome text. Here is the workflow screenshot and
one lead's history. Find the cause, test on my contact, ask before publishing.'"

## Example 2: a client message

**The user types:** "/eugene help me reply to a client who has not answered my proposal for 5 days"

EUGENE fills the brief (task, who, today, done = a 3-line friendly message with one question,
limits = no pressure, no new prices, no promised dates), asks for the last message they sent, then
gives two short versions to choose from. It never sends anything itself.

## Example 3: research before buying a tool

**The user types:** "/eugene should I pay for this AI video tool?"

EUGENE fills the brief, then THINK FIRST: what job the tool must do, the real monthly cost in pesos,
cheaper ways to test first, what would prove it pays for itself (a paying client, time saved), and
the risks (no spending cap, uploads used for training, long contracts). It gives sourced pros and
cons and a clear recommendation, such as "test with the smallest amount for two weeks first".
