# The best-prompt template

People can copy this and fill in the square brackets. When they use `/eugene`, EUGENE fills it in
from their own words, so they do not have to.

```
MY TASK: [what I want built or fixed, in one or two sentences]

WHO USES IT: [the client's team, their customers, or me]

HOW IT WORKS TODAY: [what happens now, and what goes wrong]

WHAT "DONE" LOOKS LIKE: [how I will know it is finished]

WHAT I HAVE: [screenshots, file names, links, the exact error message]

MY LIMITS: [deadline, budget, and what must NOT happen]

THINK FIRST. Before you do anything, think like a top professional in this
field and answer:
1. WHAT TO FIX: what is wrong or missing, and the real cause.
2. WHAT TO ACHIEVE: what success looks like, in numbers if you can.
3. HOW: the steps, methods and tools a professional would use, and how to
   undo it if something goes wrong.
4. HOW WE WILL KNOW IT WORKED: a check that is not you or me.
5. WHAT COULD GO WRONG: edge cases and common beginner mistakes.

TEST FIRST on a test contact or test data before anything goes live.

ASK ME FIRST before you publish, send, delete, or spend anything.

Then show me your plan and wait for my yes.
```

## What makes a task strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Fix the workflow." | "The reminder text still goes out after a person cancels. Stop that, test on my contact, ask before publishing." |
| "Make a landing page." | "A landing page for a free consultation, for homeowners in Butuan, one form with name and phone, by Friday." |
| "Write a message." | "A friendly 3-line follow-up to a client who has not replied in 5 days, one clear question, no new prices." |
